About TRAVELRAYZ

More Than Just Travel. Experiences That Bring People Together.

TRAVELRAYZ was created with a simple belief: some of life's best moments happen when we step away from our everyday routines, explore new places, and connect with people and experiences that leave a lasting impact.

We curate thoughtfully planned treks, group tours, weekend getaways, spiritual journeys, and corporate experiences for people who want more than just a destination. Whether it is watching a sunrise after a trek, sharing stories during a road journey, discovering a new place, or helping a team connect beyond the workplace, we believe every journey has the power to create meaningful memories.

At TRAVELRAYZ, our focus is on creating experiences that are engaging, well-coordinated, and memorable, from the moment a journey is planned until everyone returns home with stories to share.

Our Team

The People Behind TRAVELRAYZ

TRAVELRAYZ is built by two professionals from different backgrounds, a Software Engineer and an HR Manager, brought together by a shared passion for travel, people, and meaningful experiences.

Our professional experiences bring different strengths to TRAVELRAYZ. A technology-driven approach helps us focus on planning, organization, and smooth coordination, while an understanding of people, employee engagement, and team dynamics helps us create experiences that encourage connection and bring people together.

Together, we combine planning with passion, structure with creativity, and travel with meaningful human experiences.

Our goal is simple:

To create journeys that people don't just visit, but truly experience and remember.

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To inspire meaningful connections, healthier workplaces, and stronger teams by helping people reconnect with nature, with each other, and with themselves through transformative travel experiences.

Our Mission

To design safe, engaging, and purpose-driven experiences that foster team bonding, employee well-being, personal growth, and work-life balance through curated adventures, retreats, and corporate outings.

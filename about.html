Our Story

Crafted for seekers of peace & adventure

TRAVELRAYZ was born from a simple belief: travel should nourish both body and soul. Based in Maharashtra, we design premium group journeys across India — from sacred Jyotirlinga yatras to weekend treks and family escapes.

What started as small group departures has grown into a trusted travel community of 5,000+ happy travelers. Every itinerary reflects our commitment to safety, devotion and genuine hospitality.

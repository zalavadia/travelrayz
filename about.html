Who We Are

More than trips — we build connections

As working professionals ourselves, we understand the pressures of modern work life. TRAVELRAYZ was born from a shared belief that travel, nature, and meaningful experiences help people reconnect — with themselves, their colleagues, and the world around them.

From Mumbai, we design safe, well-planned journeys: corporate treks, team outings, digital detox retreats, offsites, sacred yatras, and weekend escapes. Every itinerary is built to strengthen teams, restore well-being, and leave lasting memories.

We create meaningful experiences that help teams:

  • Connect beyond the workplace
  • Strengthen team bonds
  • Recharge with digital detox
  • Grow through new experiences
  • Inspire healthier work-life balance

Our Approach

How We Create Meaningful Experiences

Every organization is unique — we don’t offer one-size-fits-all trips.

  1. Understand

    We begin by understanding your organization’s objectives, team dynamics, group size, budget, and desired outcomes to ensure every experience aligns with your goals.

  2. Curate

    Based on your requirements, we thoughtfully design customized experiences — whether it’s a corporate trek, digital detox program, team-building activity, offsite, or leadership retreat.

  3. Execute

    From planning and logistics to safety and on-ground coordination, our team manages every detail to deliver a seamless and hassle-free experience.

  4. Transform

    The result is more than just an outing. We create opportunities for teams to connect, collaborate, recharge, and build lasting relationships beyond the workplace.

Every organization is unique. That’s why we don’t offer one-size-fits-all trips — we curate experiences that inspire connection, strengthen teams, and create lasting impact.

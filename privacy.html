Privacy Policy

How TRAVELRAYZ collects, uses and protects your information.

Last updated: July 2026

Introduction

TRAVELRAYZ ("we", "us", "our") operates the website travelrayz.com and provides travel booking services for treks, tours and spiritual yatras. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle personal information when you visit our website, submit enquiries or communicate with us via phone, email or WhatsApp.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Contact details — name, phone number and email address provided through enquiry forms, newsletter signup or direct messages.
  • Booking information — trip preferences, travel dates, group size and payment-related details shared during booking.
  • Communication records — messages sent via WhatsApp, email or phone for customer support and trip coordination.
  • Technical data — browser type, device information, IP address and pages visited, collected automatically through standard web server logs and cookies.

How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Respond to enquiries and process trip bookings.
  • Send trip confirmations, itineraries and important travel updates.
  • Improve our website, services and customer experience.
  • Send newsletter updates about upcoming trips (only if you subscribe).
  • Comply with legal obligations and resolve disputes.

Forms & WhatsApp

When you submit our enquiry or booking form, your details are used to open a WhatsApp conversation with our team so we can assist you promptly. We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

Cookies

Our website may use local storage for admin settings and to improve browsing experience. You can clear site data in your browser settings, though some features may not function as intended.

Third-Party Services

We use third-party services that may collect information as described in their own policies:

  • Google Fonts — for typography delivery.
  • Google Maps — embedded map on our contact page.
  • Google Sheets / Apps Script — for trip data used on the site.
  • WhatsApp — for customer communication when you initiate contact.

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of these services independently.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration or disclosure. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data Retention

We retain personal information only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, comply with legal requirements or resolve disputes. Booking records may be kept for accounting and legal compliance purposes.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us. You may also unsubscribe from our newsletter at any time by replying to any email or contacting us directly.

Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Family trips may include minors, but booking information is provided by a parent or guardian.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. Continued use of our website after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
